Replace the Agent Definition-bound Session engine with native Sessions and whole-function Turns. `makeSession({ persistence })` allocates a fresh scoped Session, with an explicit `SessionStore` or `"none"`; `Session.run(program)` runs any Effect as one Turn, preserving its result, error and requirement types, and the ambient `Turn` stages Messages that commit once after the program, its cleanup and any save succeed. The Turn also exposes its owning Session, drains the program's child fibers before its Scope closes and children forked by its cleanup before commit (a finalizer must join a child that needs resources later finalizers release), and uncertain, compound or empty save failures fence the Session with `SessionFencedError`. Sessions without persistence add `runWithReceipt` for typed owner-local reads of a Turn's live outcome, and `observe(capacity)` keeps each observer's newest snapshots with sequence numbers that expose loss. `createSession`, `createHostSession`, `CreateSessionOptions` and `TurnOptions` are removed.
